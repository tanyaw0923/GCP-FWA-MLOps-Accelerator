# Synthetic demonstration investigation knowledge base

## High acuity coding
A high share of high-acuity codes or high observed-to-expected acuity ratio can be a review signal. Compare with appropriate specialty, case mix, coding documentation, and peer groups. It is not proof of fraud.

## Referrals
High referral rates may reflect patient need or clinical workflows. Examine applicable referral policies and verified records; do not infer intent from aggregate features.

## Risk score interpretation
In the current Random Forest postprocessing path fraud_score may be a binary predicted class (0 or 1), not a calibrated risk probability. A risk label is a model output, not a confirmed fraud outcome. Reason-code features are comparative signals, not causal explanations.

## Investigation process
Verify provider identity and record freshness. Review permitted supporting documentation, claims coding, and known investigation outcomes with an authorized investigator. Document evidence and human decisions; do not automate enforcement from the assistant's answer.
